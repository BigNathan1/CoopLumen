Frontend Design (Dark Mode)

Dashboard / App Page: A central workspace for viewing wallet balances, portfolio information, reputation, communities, loans, and recent account activity.
FAQ Page: A structured help section addressing common questions about Stellar, wallet usage, community finance, security, testnet access, and project updates.
Newsletter & Testnet Waitlist: A signup experience for collecting email addresses and Stellar public keys from users interested in announcements and testnet participation.
Community Detail Page: A dedicated community view presenting its assets, membership, treasury movements, loan information, reputation, and available actions.
