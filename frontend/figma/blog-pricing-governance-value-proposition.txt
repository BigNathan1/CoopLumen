Frontend Design (Dark Mode)


What's Included

1. Blog, Changelog & Release Notes Preview — #916
Adds a development updates area where visitors can follow product announcements, browse recent improvements, review release highlights, and stay informed about the project's progress.

2. Pricing & Community Plans — #911
Introduces a community plans page that communicates available participation options, contribution opportunities, and relevant benefits. It also incorporates newsletter registration, changelog access, and clear actions that encourage visitors to get involved.

3. DAO Governance & Soroban Proposals — #914
Creates a governance interface for presenting DAO proposals and their voting information. The design highlights proposal status, voting weights, quorum progress, snapshot information, and the planned role of Soroban in governance.

4. Three-Pillar Value Proposition — #913
Establishes Issue, Lend, and Govern as CoopLumen's three central community finance pillars, making the relationship between these capabilities easier to understand through a focused visual presentation.
