# Design

All CoopLumen screens are designed in one Figma file, in light and dark mode:
[Coop-Lumen-Designs](https://www.figma.com/design/JjGsxDKrypd1f3svC9xWaz/Coop-Lumen-Designs).

Implement against the design for the screen you are building, and use the
tokens in `frontend/src/app/globals.css` rather than raw colours or spacing.
