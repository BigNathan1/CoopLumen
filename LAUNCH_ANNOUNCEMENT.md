#### **CoopLumen Launches On Stellar Mainnet: Decentralized Community Finance Now Live**

Decentralized community finance platform brings peer-to-peer lending, community tokens, and on-chain governance to underserved communities worldwide.

**[CITY, COUNTRY] – [DATE]** — [Organization] today announced the launch of **CoopLumen** on Stellar Mainnet, making decentralized community finance accessible to any group: cooperatives, NGOs, diaspora networks, or local savings circles.

Built on the Stellar network, CoopLumen enables communities to:

- **Issue community tokens** — Create and manage custom assets without intermediaries
- **Govern collectively** — Vote on proposals and manage shared treasuries on-chain
- **Lend peer-to-peer** — Offer and request loans with transparent, on-chain settlement
- **Transfer value** — Send remittances and disburse funds with near-zero fees

"Traditional finance excludes 1.4 billion unbanked people. Stellar's 5-second finality and near-zero fees make it possible for any community to build their own financial infrastructure," said [Founder/Lead]. "CoopLumen removes the technical barriers — users just need a wallet and an internet connection."

### Key Features

**Community Tokens**
Members issue and exchange governance tokens that represent membership and voting power. Tokens are native Stellar assets, tradeable on the Stellar DEX and safe on any compatible wallet.

**On-Chain Governance**
CoopLumen's Soroban smart contract powers token-weighted proposal voting. Proposals are permanent records on the Stellar ledger, with voting periods, quorum requirements, and execution records immutably recorded.

**Peer-to-Peer Lending**
Members can request loans and offer capital. Agreements are transparent, repayments tracked on-chain, and disputes resolved through community governance.

**Simple User Experience**
No private key management. Users connect with [Freighter wallet](https://freighter.app), sign transactions, and manage communities through an intuitive web dashboard.

### Getting Started

Communities can launch today:

1. **Create an account** — Sign up with Freighter wallet at [cooplumen.org](https://cooplumen.org)

## FAQ for Launch

**Q: Is CoopLumen live on Testnet yet?**  
A: Yes. We've been operating on Stellar Testnet for [timeframe] with [X] communities and [Y] members. Mainnet launch represents the next phase of growth.

**Q: Is my data secure?**  
A: CoopLumen underwent an independent security audit covering smart contracts, APIs, and infrastructure. All findings were addressed before Mainnet launch. See our audit report: [link].

**Q: Do I need cryptocurrency experience?**  
A: No. CoopLumen is designed for non-technical users. Sign in with Freighter wallet, and the interface guides you through creating a community and issuing tokens.

**Q: Can I use CoopLumen on my phone?**  
A: CoopLumen is a web app and works on any device with a web browser and the Freighter wallet extension. Mobile browser support is coming soon.

**Q: What if something goes wrong?**  
A: Our on-call team monitors the system 24/7. We have incident response and rollback plans. Contact support@cooplumen.org for urgent issues.

**Q: What does CoopLumen cost?**  
A: CoopLumen is free. You only pay Stellar network fees (fractions of a cent per transaction).

**Q: Can my community use CoopLumen?**  
A: Yes. CoopLumen is open to any group. Get started at cooplumen.org.
